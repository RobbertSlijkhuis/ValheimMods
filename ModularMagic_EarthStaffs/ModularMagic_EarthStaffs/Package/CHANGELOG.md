### 0.0.4
- The staff config, update helpers and patches are now shared through ModularMagic_Core. The entries of the staff config have the same names as the other staff mods (for example Crafting costs, Upgrade costs, Attack damage blunt), so the values of the Earth staffs have to be set again in the config
- The damage of summoned creatures scales with the staff that summoned them: the damage entries of the Summon config are replaced by a Damage multiplier, the damage of the creature weapon is set in its prefab
- The Rain boulders get the damage of the staff that cast the rain instead of the damage stored in their prefab
- Fixed the terrain edit of the nova, it now happens when the spikes come up
